< About Me />

TB

I study what happens to work when machines get smarter — and I teach people how to lead through it.

Based in Melbourne, I'm an Associate Lecturer at RMIT University's School of Management, where I coordinate and teach MBA and Masters courses on AI & Workforce Transformation, Technology & Innovation, People Analytics, and Digital Entrepreneurship. I also teach Human Resource Analytics at UNSW.

My PhD research at RMIT's School of Economics examines automation, task reallocation, and sub-occupational labour market dynamics — using machine learning on large-scale job advertisement data to surface how work is quietly being reorganised beneath the surface of traditional occupational categories.

Before academia, I led Learning & Development and Organisational Development teams at Telstra, Accenture, and KPMG — where I built some of Australia's first AI-assisted workforce readiness systems, VR retail environments, and data-driven talent platforms. I bring that industry scar tissue into every classroom.

< Research />

My research sits at the intersection of labour economics, automation, and organisational behaviour. Using clustering methods on large-scale job advertisement data, I examine how occupational tasks are being quietly redistributed — often in ways that aggregate statistics miss entirely.

I am currently completing my PhD thesis at RMIT and preparing empirical work for submission to journals including Labour Economics and Organization Science.
