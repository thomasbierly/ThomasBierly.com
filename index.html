I'm Thomas Bierly — a researcher, builder, and thinker operating at the bleeding edge of artificial intelligence and its implications for the future of work.

My work sits at a critical intersection: understanding how AI systems reshape industries, transform job markets, and redefine what it means to be a knowledge worker in the 21st century. I don't just study these changes — I build tools and frameworks that help organizations navigate them.

Whether I'm training models, writing research papers, or prototyping new applications, I bring a hacker's mindset: move fast, question assumptions, and always look for the elegant solution hiding in plain sight.
